The production signal

How a model earns its place

About 1,000 candidates are tested on the last five years of data. To be chosen, a model must win on data it has never seen.

70%20%10%

Five years of data, oldest to newest

  1. 70%

    Learn

    The models are fitted on this part.

  2. 20%

    Test

    Walk-forward, about one year. A winner must improve in both halves of it.

  3. 10%

    Prove

    The newest six months are never used to fit a model. They are scored after the choice.

05Proof

Tested on history, proven on demo accounts, watched every day. Every figure below carries its date and its limits.

  1. 01

    Backtest

    Walk-forward on five years of data.

  2. 02

    Paper desk

    A simulated book, recorded live since 27 Sep 2026.

  3. 03

    IG demo accounts

    Two accounts trading unattended since 16 Oct 2026.

  4. 04

    Live account

    Only on an explicit, per-trade command. Four independent locks keep the robot away from real money.

Real money comes last: every model climbs these four steps.

Even a chosen model is re-checked every day

  1. 1%

    Risk comes first

    Every position is sized so that hitting its stop costs about 1% of the account, and the target is twice the stop. Stops are not guaranteed, so a price gap can cost more.

  2. 63

    A daily tripwire

    Each model’s last 63 calls are compared with what testing showed. A hit rate below the median turns AMBER; far below turns RED.

  3. 2,000

    The coin-flip test

    Every book is ranked against 2,000 books that keep its timing, size, stops and costs but flip a coin for direction. Skill, or luck?

One engine, three kinds of account

All three in profit as of 9 Nov 2026, all at 1% risk per trade. These are short records.

Real money · gold
+10.97%

Gold on a real-money IG account since 8 Jul 2026: 62 trades, 67.7% won, deepest drawdown −3.32%.

Pro-forma: nine deposits are removed and each trade’s result is applied to a constant capital base.

IG demo accounts
+7.9%

Two demo accounts trading every call unattended since 16 Oct 2026: 123 trades, 64% won, profit factor 1.66.

Simulated money at a real broker, after funding and fees.

Paper desk
+15.5%

Recorded live since 27 Sep 2026: 86 trades, 66% won, deepest drawdown −4.3%.

The whole book since 1 Jun is +25.4%, but June to September was replayed afterwards.

  1. 60 / 60

    Simulation matches the broker

    On the 60 calls that both the demo accounts and the paper desk traded, the outcome had the same sign every time, within 0.10R per trade.

  2. 98%

    Better than chance, so far

    The 67 paper-desk trades opened live since 6 Oct 2026 beat 98% of 2,000 coin-flip books.

  3. 64 · 65%

    Consistent execution

    Two demo accounts given the same calls won 64% and 65% of their trades. That shows the automation is reliable; it is not a second proof of the edge.

What is not proven yet.

As of 9 Nov 2026, and how we will settle it.

0.51

A small edge per call

On six unseen months the average AUC is about 0.51, not yet statistically different from chance. The returns come from many small edges under tight risk control, not from big predictions.

5

Five live-recorded weeks

The gain came from the 1-day calls. The 2- and 4-day calls and EUR/USD show no edge yet, and the replayed June to September months alone are no better than chance.

330

The forward test

Models, cut-offs and book rules stay frozen. About 330 one-day calls, roughly five months, will confirm the edge or reject it.

Nothing on this page is a promise of future returns.
