03How it works

From market data
to a decision.

Six automatic steps run every morning, the same way every day. No gut feel, no forgotten stops.

  1. 01

    Data

    Prices, macro and news for 58 symbols, from six data sources.

  2. 02

    Features

    180–270 inputs per market, built without looking ahead.

  3. 03

    Models

    A team of three models per market, chosen on data they have never seen.

  4. 04

    Signals

    Long, short or wait, each with a stop and a target set before entry.

  5. 05

    Trading

    A paper desk and two IG demo accounts trade every call.

  6. 06

    Reports

    A morning brief by e-mail, and every dashboard up to date.

The run starts at 08:00 Ulaanbaatar time, when the daily candle closes. The brief arrives at 08:40.

The platform

It all runs on Brainy, our own research platform.

Clients and partners use its desks for the daily calls, paper trading, options and technical reads, in the browser or through one API.
