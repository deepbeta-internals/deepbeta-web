The production signal

How a model earns its place

About 1,000 candidates are tested on the last five years of data. To be chosen, a model must win on data it has never seen.

70%20%10%

Five years of data, oldest to newest

  1. 70%

    Learn

    The models are fitted on this part.

  2. 20%

    Test

    Walk-forward, about one year. A winner must improve in both halves of it.

  3. 10%

    Prove

    The newest six months are never used to fit a model. They are scored after the choice.

The October lesson
12 / 15

products where the proven model beat a freshly retrained one. So a new model replaces the old one only if it wins on unseen data.

For the six focus markets, each daily call comes from a team of three models, LightGBM, logistic regression and Extra-Trees, combined by rank and calibrated. A wider library is trained and compared on the same tests.

The stack underneath

Each layer is independently testable and replaceable.

  1. 01

    Data & Feature layer

    Tick / bar / fundamentals / macro / news ingest → versioned feature store with point-in-time correctness.

  2. 02

    13 ML models

    Specialists trained per family, walk-forward and purged-CV validated.

  3. 03

    Decision-making layer

    Ensembling, regime gating, signal calibration and conviction scoring.

  4. 04

    Strategy layer

    Horizon, sizing, holding-period and universe rules — per-asset and portfolio.

  5. 05

    Risk management

    Organization-specific constrained RM (mandate / exposure / liquidity caps) plus standard RM (VaR, drawdown, vol-target, correlation, stress).

  6. 06

    Execution layer

    SOTA execution with adaptive scheduling, slippage modeling, smart routing and venue selection.

  7. 07

    Mitigation & monitoring

    Live drift detection, kill-switches, circuit breakers and post-trade attribution.

03Principles

What we hold
ourselves to.

  1. 01

    Evidence before belief

    Every model is trained independently, validated walk-forward and ranked on Sharpe, Calmar, drawdown and return. Nothing ships on a good story.

  2. 02

    Risk before return

    Mandate, exposure and liquidity limits are built into the engine as constraints, alongside VaR, drawdown, volatility-target, correlation and stress controls.

  3. 03

    Open by default

    Every desk has an API and every route is documented. The paper book in Results is exported from the Paper Desk, with spread and funding costs already deducted.

  4. 04

    Replaceable by design

    Each layer, from the feature store to execution, can be tested and swapped on its own. No black box you have to take whole.
